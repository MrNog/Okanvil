# ICC spell ids by difficulty

The same ability carries a DIFFERENT id at each difficulty, which is why a
note copied from someone else's pack can sit there doing nothing: the line is
waiting on a spell this raid size never casts.

Read from three complete logs by matching boss names in the combat log -- these
are ids that were OBSERVED being cast, not ids that merely exist in the client.
25 heroic is missing; a column marked `--` means the ability was not seen at
that difficulty, which for a heroic-only mechanic is the correct answer.

The two oozes are easy to mix up, and they want opposite things from the raid:

- **Volatile Ooze Adhesive** roots one player with a green beam. The raid
  stacks ON that player to split the explosion. A Blessing of Protection
  clears the adhesive, which drops the tether and frees them to dps again.
- **Gaseous Bloat** is the other ooze: it chases one player, who kites it
  while the stacks tick up. Nobody stacks on this one.

`SPELL_AURA_APPLIED` carries the target name, so an aura can name the
player to stack on without anyone having to call it out.
