# ⚒️ Okanvil — the RATS raid addon

> *Okanvil is where all the tools are forged.* 🔨

**Okanvil** *("OK Anvil")* is the guild's own addon, forged by Okanor for WotLK 3.3.5a.

## Why it exists

Raid nights ran on a pile of addons, spreadsheets and "who had that again?" on voice.
Loot council lived in officer chat, notes were pasted by hand, and the website
never knew what really dropped. Okanvil puts it in one place, built around how
**RATS** actually raids.

## What it does for you

- 🏆 **Loot Council.** When an item drops you get a popup: **BIS, Upgrade, Small, OS or Pass**.
  Officers see every answer next to the guild's loot priority, so decisions are fair and fast.
  **Without the addon, the council can't see you.**
- 📜 **Raid notes.** Your boss assignments switch on as you walk into each room, and the
  guild WeakAuras read them for you.
- 📸 **Raid snapshots.** Who was in each raid, for attendance on the website.
- 🧰 **Extras, if you want them:** Raid Finder, PuG builder, combat logging, ID Finder, Farm tracker.

It stays quiet in combat, and officer tools only show up for officers.

## Get it

1. Download **Okanvil.zip**: https://github.com/MrNog/Okanvil/releases/latest
2. Unzip into `World of Warcraft\Interface\AddOns`
3. Restart WoW. A short setup walks you through the rest.

Stuck? Ask an officer for a version check. It shows who has Okanvil, and they can whisper you the link. 🐀🧀
